About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture reminders, ideas, and tasks. Add a note whenever inspiration strikes and keep the things you need to remember close at hand.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and find the “Add a note” form.
  2. Type a reminder or idea into the New note field.
  3. Select “Add Note” to add it to your notes list.

Features

  • Quickly capture tasks and ideas.
  • See your notes together in one list.
  • Use a simple, distraction-free interface.

Keyboard shortcuts
